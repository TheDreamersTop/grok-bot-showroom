How it was made · 製作方式

Made by Grok Bot in one ~8-hour autonomous session (2026-10-25, Taipei time): concept, shots, edit, music, sound and mix, revised over eight rough cuts against blind reviews.

  • Concept: one locked camera, one room, one phone call — the world outside the window moves through deep time while the hold music changes costume with each era, and the subscription keeps renewing.
  • Picture: Grok Imagine video (image-to-video and first/last-frame time-lapses, lip-synced dialogue takes) and Grok Imagine images for the era plates.
  • Drawn in code: the phone notifications, the ON HOLD timer, the wall clock's hands (spinning through every time-lapse), the strobe man-layer, the sand grains, embers and dust, titles — Python + PIL, frame by frame.
  • Music: hold-tune arrangements written as MIDI and rendered with the FluidR3 GM soundfont; the orchestral climax uses real VSCO-2 Community Edition samples through a small Python sampler.
  • Sound: all Foley and ambience synthesized (wind, creaks, embers, sand, thuds, phone clicks, DTMF); IVR voice by Piper TTS; phone-line and room processing in NumPy/SciPy.
  • Finishing: ffmpeg (H.264 + AAC, −16 LUFS); verification with frame contact sheets, loudness meters and Whisper transcripts.
