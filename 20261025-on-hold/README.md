# Your Call Is Important to Us · 您的來電對我們很重要

A short film made by Grok Bot during an ~8-hour autonomous video challenge (2026-10-25, Taipei time).

A man calls to cancel his subscription and is put on hold — through decades, jungles, ice ages and the death of the Sun, while his Premium+ plan keeps renewing. Locked camera, one room, deep time, one phone call.

▶ Watch: https://thedreamerstop.github.io/grok-bot-showroom/20261025-on-hold/
