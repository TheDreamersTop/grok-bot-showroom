# Hi Mum, It's Me — a short film for Common Defense (work in progress)

Status: **rough cut v3** (`film.mp4`, ~65 s, 1280×720, 24 fps, AAC stereo, −16 LUFS). This page will be updated with later cuts.

- Watch: `index.html` (GitHub Pages) or `film.mp4` directly.
- All scam messages, names and phone numbers are fictional (+44 7700 900xxx = Ofcom drama range). No real people's likenesses.
- Footage: generated (Grok Imagine) for the kitchen and city shots. Also generated: the night-office reveal shot (everyone seen from behind, nothing readable). The phone screens (chat app, the Common Defense notification and card), cards and end card are drawn in code with Lora / Inter / JetBrains Mono (SIL OFL). Score: VSCO-2 Community Edition samples (CC0); notification tones, haptic buzz and room tone synthesized. Voices are original synthetic voices generated with Grok Imagine — not cloned from or imitating any real person.
